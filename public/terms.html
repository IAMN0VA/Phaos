Permitted purpose

Use the application for visualization, education, and exploration of imaging data. Do not rely on it for diagnosis, emergency decisions, treatment decisions, or as a substitute for the original medical record or radiology report.

Your data

You are responsible for having authority to open, view, and share any study you select. Avoid identifiable data on devices or networks that you do not control.

Reference anatomy

Reference anatomy features provide approximate atlas-based locations. They are not patient-specific detections unless a future feature explicitly states that a patient-specific segmentation has been loaded and validated.

Availability and compatibility

DICOM studies vary widely by modality, transfer syntax, acquisition geometry, and vendor. Unsupported or malformed studies may not render. Always retain the original source files and clinical report.

Changes

Version and build information is shown in the About panel. Production deployments should publish release notes and a support contact.
