Visualization and education only. SCAN//SPACE is not intended for diagnosis, triage, treatment selection, treatment monitoring, emergency decision-making, or primary clinical interpretation.

Purpose

The application helps users explore the spatial relationship between medical-image slices and a rendered volume, inspect image geometry, view deterministic intensity presets, place markers, and understand predefined anatomical references.

Anatomy references

Anatomy search, Circle & Identify, focus, and reference isolation use predefined anatomical reference locations. They do not prove that a particular patient structure was detected. A reference result must not be interpreted as a segmentation, diagnosis, abnormality assessment, or statement that an organ is normal.

Measurements and voxel values

Measurements use DICOM world coordinates when valid spatial calibration is available. CT voxel probing may expose Hounsfield-unit values supplied by the decoded dataset. These functions are for visualization and educational exploration and require independent validation before any clinical use.

Clinical interpretation

Radiologists and qualified health professionals remain responsible for medical interpretation. If a scan or report raises a health concern, use the original radiology report and appropriate clinical care rather than SCAN//SPACE as a diagnostic source.

Regulatory status

This build does not represent itself as a licensed or authorized medical device. Before marketing or deploying it for a regulated medical purpose, the operator must obtain an appropriate regulatory classification and any required authorization in the relevant jurisdiction.
