Educational visualization only. This software is not intended for diagnosis, treatment, treatment planning, patient monitoring, triage, emergency decisions, clinical decision-making, primary diagnostic interpretation, equipment calibration, acceptance testing, or certified quality control.

Intended audiences

PHAOS is designed for members of the public, medical and health-science students, educators, and imaging-industry partners or customers using it as a supplemental educational or non-clinical technical-visualization resource.

Explore

Explore helps a user visualize medical imaging in 2D and 3D and understand how image slices, anatomical orientation, and spatial relationships fit together. If a user opens their own medical images, the application is not a substitute for the official radiology report or medical interpretation by an appropriate healthcare professional.

Study

Study supports anatomy and medical-imaging education using image data. It may be used to learn axial, sagittal, and coronal orientation; relationships between slices and volumes; anatomical terminology; CT window concepts; intensity or voxel values; measurements; and predefined anatomical references. Reference anatomy is educational and is not a statement that a patient-specific structure has been detected or segmented.

Demo

Demo supports imaging-equipment companies and their clients in visualizing scanner-generated DICOM, demonstrating imaging output, exploring interoperability, and using imaging as an educational resource. Demo is not intended to certify device performance, calibrate equipment, perform acceptance testing, establish diagnostic image quality, or replace validated manufacturer or regulatory quality-control procedures.

Viewer functions

The software can display DICOM and ordinary image formats, reconstruct volumetric image series, provide multiplanar views, apply deterministic display presets, show voxel or CT intensity information where available, make image-space measurements, place markers, and compare a circled location with a predefined anatomical reference catalog. These functions are provided for the intended educational and demonstration purposes above.

Anatomical references

Anatomy search, Circle & Identify, focus, and reference isolation use predefined reference locations and deterministic spatial rules. Results such as Near, Moderate, or Distant describe proximity to a reference location. They are not diagnostic confidence scores and do not prove that a patient structure, lesion, disease, or abnormality was detected.

Measurements and voxel values

Measurements may use DICOM world coordinates when valid spatial calibration is available. CT voxel probing may expose values supplied by the decoded dataset. These tools are educational visualization features in this release and are not represented as independently validated clinical measurements or equipment-certification measurements.

Regulatory boundary

This release does not represent itself as a licensed or authorized medical device. If the intended use is later expanded to diagnosis, treatment planning, clinical decision-making, primary interpretation, equipment certification, formal quality assurance, or another regulated medical purpose, the product should undergo a separate regulatory and validation review before that use is promoted or distributed.
