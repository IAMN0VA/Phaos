Partner distribution does not convert this release into a diagnostic or certified quality-control product.

Appropriate partner uses

An imaging-equipment manufacturer, distributor, educational organization, or other partner may use the application to demonstrate scanner-generated DICOM, visualize image output in 2D/3D, explain spatial relationships, teach imaging concepts, explore non-clinical interoperability, and provide customers with a supplemental educational visualization resource.

How to describe it

Recommended wording: “A supplemental educational and technical-visualization resource for exploring imaging output. Not intended for diagnostic interpretation, clinical decision-making, equipment calibration, acceptance testing, or certified quality control.”

Claims to avoid for this release

Do not state or imply that the application validates, certifies, approves, calibrates, quality-assures, or establishes the diagnostic performance of an imaging device. Do not describe it as a diagnostic workstation, PACS replacement, clinical decision-support system, or tool for detecting pathology.

Demonstration and client datasets

Partners should use de-identified or synthetic demonstration studies whenever practical. A partner that provides identifiable customer or patient data must independently ensure that its collection, use, disclosure, access controls, contracts, and privacy notices satisfy applicable requirements.

Changes by a partner

If a partner adds remote image transfer, PACS connectivity, user accounts, cloud storage, telemetry, clinical reporting, automated interpretation, equipment-testing logic, or other functionality, the intended-use, privacy, cybersecurity, and regulatory assessment must be revisited before distribution of that modified deployment.

Co-branding

Co-branded websites, manuals, conference material, sales material, and client communications should use consistent approved intended-use language. A disclaimer inside the viewer does not correct contradictory external marketing claims.

Future regulated versions

A future version may be separately developed and validated for clinical or formal equipment-quality workflows. That would be treated as a distinct product scope and should not be inferred from this educational release.
