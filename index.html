PROTOTYPE FORMAT SUPPORT

Uncompressed 8/16-bit grayscale DICOM (Implicit/Explicit VR Little Endian), plus browser-readable PNG/JPEG. Individual .dcm files can be reconstructed into a volume, and ZIP archives containing DICOM can be unpacked locally when the browser supports ZIP decompression APIs. Compressed DICOM transfer syntaxes are still intentionally rejected in this zero-dependency prototype.
